# Glossary

**Post**: a dated article in the Blog. Belongs to exactly one Category.

**Category**: the single subject a Post is filed under (python, self-host, hardware, java, life, linux). Decides the Post's tag colour and its Ramp.

**Project** (shown as a *cart*): a small tool that runs in the browser on its own page. May carry Project tags.

**Project tag**: a label grouping Projects (haiku, SFC). Unrelated to Categories.

**Song**: a published audio track with a cover image, listed on the Music page.

**Ramp**: one of five named four-colour sets (night, ember, copper, sea, moss) drawn from the 16-colour palette. Every photo shown on the site is reduced to one Ramp.

**Palette**: the fixed 16 PICO-8 colours. Nothing on the site uses a colour outside it.

**Band**: a thin dithered strip used as a section divider.

**Sprite**: a small pixel-art icon (the snail, section icons, date icons) drawn on a 16px grid and shown only at whole-number scales.
